# Scan Surprise

**I've gotten bored of handing out flags as text. Wouldn't it be cool if they were an image instead?**

Nos conectamos al servidor que nos da picoCTF, vemos que contiene el siguiente QR:

<img width="529" height="584" alt="Screenshot_20260621_201908" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/12de964f-524b-49ba-80f9-6431488798de" />

Escaneamos el QR con nuestro celular o con el comando:

**zbarimg flag.png**

y obtenemos la flag:

**picoCTF{p33k_@_b00_b5ce2572}**

Subimos la flag a picoctf y listo.
